## Starting Swarm Project 

Based on shared interest in RL and swarming, together with a few more researchers at my institution, we have decided to focus our efforts on pursuing research in emergent drone swarms, a.k.a. building multi-agent environments.

Our goal was to create an implementation that allows us to develop algorithms in simulation and then test them in the physical world, bridging the [simulation-to-reality gap](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212827122007004). Additionally, we wanted a platform that could be modeled at varying levels of precision. Ideally, it should support both a simple point-in-space representation and behavior that translates well to the real world—a perfect starting point.

Naturally, drones such as quadcopters or n-copters became the platform of choice for us.

They can be modeled with different degrees of detail, ranging from a point in space controlled by a thrust vector to a full physics-based model with separate force vectors for each motor. Since a drone is essentially a rigid body that can also be approximated as a “floating sphere/point” in the physical world, it provides an excellent foundation for our research.

It is extremely easy to buy multiple drones in batches, and currently technology as a robotic platform seems to be mature enough to allow for different experimentation for us as researchers. 
## Aim 

We have two key requirements:

1. **Compatibility with Gym** - similar to [EvolutionGym](https://evolutiongym.github.io/)
2. **Flexibility and extensibility** -to easily support new drone models, allow modifications, and make the platform reusable for other research groups.
